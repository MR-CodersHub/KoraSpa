Deep Tissue Massage

For those carrying the weight of chronic tension or recovering from physical exertion, our Deep Tissue Massage delivers firm, purposeful pressure to reach the deeper layers of muscle and connective tissue. This treatment is especially beneficial for athletes, those with desk-related postural strain, or anyone holding persistent muscular knots.

Your therapist will apply sustained, focused strokes and cross-fibre friction techniques to break down adhesions and restore freedom of movement. The result: meaningful relief that lasts for days, not hours.

From: $95

  • Improves posture and corrects muscular imbalances

Aromatherapy Massage

Transport your senses to a world of botanical beauty with our Aromatherapy Massage — a multi-sensory experience that pairs the healing power of touch with the transformative properties of pure essential oils. At the start of each session, your therapist will invite you to select a custom scent profile tailored to your mood: calming lavender and chamomile, uplifting citrus and bergamot, or grounding cedarwood and frankincense.

The gentle massage strokes encourage the essential oils to penetrate the skin while their aromatic molecules work through the olfactory system to elevate mood, ease anxiety, and gently rebalance body and mind. A truly holistic ritual from first breath to last.

From: $90

Heat Therapy

Hot Stone Therapy

Experience the ancient healing power of heat with our Hot Stone Therapy — one of the most deeply relaxing treatments in our collection. Smooth, hand-selected basalt stones are heated to the perfect temperature and placed along the body's key energy points, while your therapist uses them to deliver flowing strokes that penetrate far deeper than hands alone.

The sustained warmth from the stones dilates blood vessels, dramatically increasing circulation while melting away layers of tension that conventional massage simply cannot reach. This is a treatment for those who crave true depth of relaxation — and those who have never experienced anything like it.

Duration: 90 min
From: $115
  • Achieves a depth of relaxation unlike any other treatment
  • Improves circulation and lymphatic drainage
  • Provides profound and lasting stress relief

Classic Favourite

Swedish Massage

The Swedish Massage is the gold standard of therapeutic bodywork — a timeless classic that has endured for good reason. Using a carefully choreographed sequence of effleurage (long gliding strokes), petrissage (kneading), tapotement (rhythmic tapping), and gentle stretching, this treatment covers the entire body systematically, leaving no area of tension untouched.

Scientifically shown to reduce cortisol, increase serotonin, and promote deeper sleep, the Swedish Massage is the ideal starting point for new spa guests and a beloved ritual for returning clients. If you're looking for comprehensive whole-body relaxation, this is your treatment.

Duration: 60 or 90 min
From: $80
  • Delivers total full-body relaxation and tension release
  • Promotes better, deeper sleep and mental clarity
  • Enhances overall sense of wellbeing and vitality
