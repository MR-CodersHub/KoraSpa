The Experience

A Sanctuary for Your Senses

From the moment you cross our threshold, every detail has been arranged to encourage complete surrender to tranquility.

Our Relaxation Massage is more than an appointment — it is a deliberate pause in an over-stimulated world. Upon arrival, you will be invited to decompress in our lounge while enjoying warm botanical water and conferring briefly with your therapist to understand your intentions for the day.

Once inside your private, softly candlelit room, the treatment begins with rhythmic, flowing strokes that gently awaken circulation and prepare muscle fibers for release. There are no sudden movements or painful pressures here; only continuous, harmonious gliding strokes calibrated to soothe your parasympathetic nervous system.

  • Warm welcome & bespoke personal consultation
  • Private candlelit suite with organic linen & ambient acoustic resonance
  • Selection of pure, cold-pressed natural massage oils
  • Gentle, rhythmic full-body coverage from neck to toes
  • Calming closing sequence with guided grounding breathwork

Pre-Treatment

Arrive 10 minutes prior to unwind in our lounge, sip herbal tea, and slip into an organic plush robe and slippers.

Immerse yourself in 55 or 85 continuous minutes of rhythmic touch, peaceful melodies, and pure restorative comfort.

Post-Treatment

Gently awaken with artisanal botanical tea, organic dried fruit, and quiet time in our garden relaxation solarium.

Before Your Visit

Essential Guest Guidelines

A few thoughtful preparations to ensure you receive the utmost benefit from your appointment.

Preparation

We recommend avoiding heavy meals for at least 2 hours prior to your session. Please arrive 10 minutes early so you can transition gracefully into our serene atmosphere without rushing.

What to Wear

We provide ultra-soft robes, plush slippers, and disposable undergarments. During your massage, you will be professionally draped at all times with only the active area exposed.

General Suitability

This gentle therapy is suitable for most adults seeking decompression. If you are pregnant, undergoing medical treatment, or have acute injuries, kindly notify our team when scheduling.

Cancellations

Should your schedule shift, please notify us at least 24 hours in advance to reschedule without penalty. Appointments cancelled with less than 24 hours notice may incur a 50% courtesy fee.

Wellness Disclaimer: Kora Spa treatments are curated wellness, relaxation, and rejuvenation services. We do not provide medical diagnosis, physiotherapy, or curative clinical interventions. Consult your primary healthcare physician for treatment of acute or chronic medical conditions.
