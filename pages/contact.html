Directions to Our Sanctuary

Conveniently situated in the heart of Manhattan’s Wellness District, easily accessible by public transit and private car.

123 Serenity Lane, Wellness District, NY 10001
Open in Google Maps

Subway Transit

Take the A, C, or E line to 42nd Street-Port Authority or 50th St Station. A calm 5-minute walk along tree-lined Serenity Lane brings you to our entrance.

Driving & Valet

Convenient access via 10th and 11th Avenues. Paid garage parking is located directly next door at 125 Serenity Lane Garage.

Bus Routes

MTA bus routes 7, 14, and 42 stop directly at Serenity Lane & 9th Ave, steps from our welcoming double doors.

Walking from Central Park

We are a scenic 10-minute walk south from Central Park South, providing the perfect tranquil stroll before or after your therapy.

Parking Notice: Complimentary valet parking is available for all clients on Saturdays and Sundays. Validated discounted self-parking stamps are provided at reception for weekday visits.
